Good to know

Questions people ask before calling

Am I speaking to a real person?
No — Ava is an AI voice assistant, and she says so when she picks up. Ask for a human at any point in the call and she'll escalate to a specialist along with a summary of what you've already told her.
Do I need my policy number ready?
No — your registered mobile number is all we ask for, and it's enough to find your policy. Ava looks it up before she says hello, so she opens by name and never makes you read numbers out loud. If the number you give isn't the registered one, she'll check who you are another way.
Can I mute myself mid-call?
Yes. The mute button stops your microphone from being sent at all — useful if someone walks in or you need to check a document. Ava keeps talking; unmute when you're ready and carry on.
What happens to the conversation?
The call is transcribed live so you can read along in the call window, and that transcript is what gets handed to a specialist if you're escalated. Audio goes only between your browser and our support service over an encrypted connection.
What if the line drops?
Anything Ava recorded during the call — a claim, for instance — already has a reference number. Call back, mention it, and she picks up from there.

Got a question about your cover?

Ava is on the line right now. Most calls are done in a couple of minutes.
